About BNL

Bridgeworks Network Limited

Bridgeworks Network Limited (BNL) is an Australian-registered organisation (ABN 82 695 253 126), working locally and globally to restore hope and transform lives through holistic care.

The name "Bridgeworks" reflects our core purpose — we build bridges between communities, between hope and despair, and between isolation and belonging. We believe that with the right support, every person has the capacity to flourish.

Our Mission

Restoring Hope, Transforming Lives

Our mission is simple but powerful: Restoring hope, transforming lives. Everything BNL does flows from this commitment — to see vulnerable communities lifted from cycles of hardship and given the tools to build a lasting future.

We believe in holistic care — addressing not just immediate needs but the deeper roots of disadvantage, including social isolation, trauma, and lack of opportunity.

Our Values

We Care

Our six core values — captured in the phrase "We Care" — guide every decision, programme, and relationship we build.

  • Wellbeing — Prioritising the health and flourishing of every person we serve
  • Empathy — Listening deeply and walking alongside those in need
  • Compassion — Acting with warmth and genuine care for human dignity
  • Accountability — Transparent and responsible stewardship of trust and resources
  • Respect — Honouring the worth of every individual, regardless of circumstances
  • Effectiveness — Striving for measurable, lasting impact in all we do
What We Do

Our Initiatives

In partnership with the Reuben Wairicu Foundation — a registered not-for-profit in Kenya — BNL supports vulnerable communities impacted by incarceration.

🇰🇪 Kenya Partnership
🏠

Community Restoration

Safe housing, healthcare and education for children living inside prisons with their parents — ensuring their future isn't defined by present circumstances.

  • Safe housing for families
  • Healthcare access
  • Children's education
🔑 Reintegration Program
🌎

Life After Prison

Skills training, mentorship and reintegration support for individuals rebuilding their lives — because everyone deserves a second chance.

  • Vocational skills training
  • Reintegration support
  • Ongoing mentorship

With limited resources, BNL relies on the generosity of individuals and organisations to bring these life-changing initiatives to life.
